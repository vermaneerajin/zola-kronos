A measure of 60–70 characters reads well on a desktop and badly on a phone,
where the same line length means a smaller type size. Kronos caps prose at
`34rem` and starts at 17px, stepping up to 18px from the tablet breakpoint.

Headings use a slightly negative letter-spacing and `text-wrap: balance`, which
keeps two-line titles from leaving a single orphaned word.
